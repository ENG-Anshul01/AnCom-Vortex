//ABOUT

ANcome VORTEX ROBOTICS is a non-profit, student-led Robotics & Technology Community associated with CSJMU, Kanpur. Its purpose: to help students learn technology through practical projects, hands-on experimentation, robotics competitions and innovation activities.

CSE

AI, ML, computer vision, robot software, web, automation, autonomous systems.

ELECTRONICS

Embedded systems, sensors, circuits, microcontrollers, motor control, hardware integration.

MECHANICAL

Robot design, fabrication, chassis, mechanisms, mechanical integration.
